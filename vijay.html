Joseph Vijay, born Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar on 22 June 1974 in Chennai, is an Indian former actor, dancer, and playback singer who is currently serving as the 9th Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu since May 2026. Popularly known as "Thalapathy" (Commander), he is the founder and president of the political party Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), which emerged as the single largest party in the 2026 Tamil Nadu assembly elections. Vijay began his career as a child artist in the 1980s before becoming one of Tamil cinema’s highest-paid and most commercially successful actors, with notable films including Ghilli (2004), Thuppakki (2012), and Mersal (2017). He announced his retirement from acting in early 2024 to enter politics, leveraging his massive fan base and social welfare organization, Vijay Makkal Iyakkam (VMI), to transition into a political career.
